# Privacy Policy for Trend Wizard

**Last updated: August 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you use the Trend Wizard application (the "App").

## 1. Information We Collect
Trend Wizard is designed to be privacy-centric. We do not require you to create a personal account, and we do not collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name or address. 

To provide our core functionalities and manage subscriptions, we utilize the following third-party services:
* **RevenueCat:** Used to manage in-app purchases and PRO subscriptions. RevenueCat generates an anonymous App User ID to validate your subscription status securely.
* **Firebase (Google Cloud):** Used as our backend infrastructure to deliver real-time data, algorithmic models, and mathematical simulations. 

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The anonymous data processed by our third-party providers is used strictly to:
* Verify and maintain your active PRO subscription status.
* Fetch the latest market data and trends to display in the App.
* Monitor general app performance and crash reports to improve stability.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.
